About Drain Jet It

Drain Jet It is a Bloemfontein-based business that provides professional drainage and high-pressure cleaning services to residential and commercial customers.

Our History

Drain Jet It has been providing drainage and cleaning solutions in the local market for several years. The organisation has advertised its services through local media and provides specialised drainage and high-pressure cleaning solutions.

Drain Jet It newspaper advertisement.

Our Mission

Our proposed mission is to provide professional, reliable and effective drainage and high-pressure cleaning solutions while prioritising customer satisfaction and environmentally responsible practices.

Our Vision

Our proposed vision is to become a trusted provider of innovative and environmentally responsible drainage and high-pressure cleaning solutions in Bloemfontein and surrounding areas.

Our Organisation

Drain Jet It provides a range of services including drain jetting, drain unblocking, root intrusion solutions, soak-away cleaning, fat-trap cleaning, roof cleaning and paving cleaning.
